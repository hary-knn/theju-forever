My dearest Theju,

I don't think you will ever fully understand how much you mean to me. Sometimes I look at us and wonder how I got so lucky to have someone like you in my life. Before you, I never knew that one person could make such a difference to the way I see everything. You became my favourite notification, my favourite conversation, my reason to smile at my phone, and the person I wish I could tell every little thing to. I know distance isn't always easy. There are days when I wish I could simply be beside you, hold your hand, look into your eyes, and tell you everything without a screen between us. Some days, I wish I could skip all these miles and just come home to you. But even on those days, I never want you to doubt what you mean to me. The distance doesn't make my feelings smaller. If anything, it reminds me how much I look forward to the little things — seeing you, hugging you, laughing with you, and making memories that don't have to end with a goodbye. I know I'm not perfect. I'll have my silly moments, my stubborn moments, and days when I don't know how to express what I feel. But I promise I'll keep trying. I'll keep listening, understanding, learning, and choosing you. I don't want a perfect love story. I want ours — real, honest, a little crazy, full of laughter, and strong enough to get through the difficult days. When I think about the future, I don't just imagine big achievements or beautiful places. I imagine the ordinary things with you. Waking up beside you, annoying you for no reason, sharing food, arguing over silly things, celebrating little wins, supporting your dreams, and building a life where we both feel loved and safe. Thank you for being my person. Thank you for every laugh, every conversation, every little effort, and every memory we've made. Thank you for being Theju. And if I had to start all over again, knowing everything I know now, I'd still find my way to you. Not just today. Not just when things are easy. I'd choose you in every lifetime.
Yours, in every lifetime ♡
