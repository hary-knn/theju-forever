My dearest Darshana,

Sometimes I wonder how one person can become such a big part of another person's world. Then I think of you, and somehow I understand. You have brought warmth into places in my heart I didn't know were still cold. You make me feel understood, you give me reasons to smile on difficult days, and you remind me that love isn't only in grand gestures — it's in the little things, in the way we talk, laugh, care, and keep choosing each other. I may not always find the perfect words, and I know I won't be perfect every day. But I want you to know this: I want to keep learning you, listening to you, standing beside you, and making space for every version of you. On the easy days, on the messy days, and on the days when all we can do is hold on a little tighter. When I imagine the future, the happiest parts are not places or things. They're the simple moments with you — sharing a home, making memories, teasing each other over silly things, supporting each other's dreams, and growing older with a love that feels safe and steady. Thank you for being you, Darshana. Thank you for letting me love you. If life asks me to choose again and again, I hope my answer will always be the same. You. Today, tomorrow, and all the days after that.
With all my love, always ♡
